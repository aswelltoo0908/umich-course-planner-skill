# Sources and operational rules

## Preferred sources

1. Official degree and department pages under `umich.edu`.
2. LSA Course Guide: `https://webapps.lsa.umich.edu/cg/`.
3. Wolverine Access for authoritative enrollment state.
4. Atlas: `https://atlas.ai.umich.edu/`.
5. Registrar academic calendar, registration deadlines, tuition, and credit-load policies.
6. Rate My Professors or similar public reviews only as secondary qualitative evidence.

## Enrollment edge cases

- A waitlist is not equivalent to no chance of enrollment; label it separately from open and closed.
- Reserved seats may prevent enrollment despite a positive open-seat count.
- Some courses require linked lecture, discussion, lab, recitation, or exam components.
- Flexible lecture attendance does not automatically make a conflicting enrolled section valid.
- Check evening exams and one-time mandatory meetings.
- Treat campus travel as part of conflict detection.
- More than 18 credits in Fall/Winter generally requires an LSA overload request and may change tuition.
- A course may satisfy a category elective without being a declaration prerequisite; state the exact bucket.

## Ranking guidance

Prefer instructor clarity, preparedness, respect, course-specific experience, and adequate sample size. Penalize advanced/honors variants when the student prioritizes GPA or workload unless the student wants the extra challenge. If assessments are standardized across sections, explain that section time and support may matter more than instructor identity.
