# Privacy notice

UMich Course Planner is an independent course-planning assistant. Its skill guides an AI assistant using the information you provide and sources available to that assistant. Its schedule-checking tool sends the course names, meeting times, optional locations and credit values supplied to the tool to an OpenAI Sites-hosted service to compute conflicts and totals. Do not include passwords, student identification numbers, personal addresses, or other sensitive details in schedule inputs.

The tool is designed to process each request without a creator-operated database, analytics integration or persistent storage. The hosting provider and the AI service may process operational data under their own policies; we do not claim they never retain logs. University websites, third-party sources, and the AI service have their own data practices. Users sign in to university services directly, rather than providing passwords, authentication codes, cookies or tokens to the plugin.

The skill aims to avoid identifying students in reusable or public outputs. Automated redaction is not guaranteed to remove every identifier; review materials before sharing. Contact aswelltoo@icloud.com for privacy questions.
